# UK Public Holidays

A website showing UK public holidays for England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland.

## Holidays

Public holidays include bank holidays and common law holidays.

Bank Holidays are defined by the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 and can be either statutory (declared in Schedule 1 of the Act) or proclaimed (by either the King or the Secretary of State for Northern Ireland under Section 1 of the Act). Days can also be proclaimed to no longer be bank holidays.

In England, Wales, and Northern Ireland Christmas and Good Friday are common law holidays.

The statutory bank holidays in England and Wales are:
* Easter Monday
* The last Monday in May
* The last Monday in August
* 26 December, if not a Sunday
* 27 December, if 25 or 26 December was a Sunday

The statutory bank holidays in Scotland are:
* New Year’s Day, if not a Sunday
* 2 January, if not a Sunday
* 3 January, if 1 or 2 January was a Sunday
* Good Friday
* The first Monday in May
* The first Monday in August
* 30 November, if not a Saturday or Sunday
* The first Monday following 30 November, if 30 November was a Saturday or Sunday
* Christmas Day, if not a Sunday
* 26 December, if Christmas Day was a Sunday

The statutory bank holidays in Northern Ireland are:
* 17 March, if not a Sunday
* 18 March, if 17 March was a Sunday
* Easter Monday
* The last Monday in May
* The last Monday in August
* 26 December, if not a Sunday
* 27 December, if 25 or 26 December was a Sunday

Proclaimed bank holidays are published in [The Gazette](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/) and parsed by [cyruscook/bankholiday_retriever](https://github.com/cyruscook/bankholiday_retriever).
